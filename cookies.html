Cookies Policy

At Yombol Music we use cookies and similar technologies to improve browsing experience, analyze traffic, and display personalized ads through services such as Google AdSense. This policy explains what cookies are, how we use them, and how you can manage them.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They are used to remember your preferences, improve site performance, and provide you with relevant content.

2. Types of cookies we use

  • Essential cookies: necessary for the basic functioning of the site.
  • Performance cookies: help us understand how users interact with the website.
  • Functionality cookies: remember your preferences and personalize your experience.
  • Advertising cookies: allow us to display relevant ads through Google AdSense and other partners.

3. Third-party cookies

Some cookies are managed by third parties, such as Google (AdSense, Analytics) or beat-selling platforms (Airbit, Beatstars). These cookies collect browsing data to provide statistics and personalized advertising.

4. Managing cookies

You can accept or reject cookies through the consent banner when entering the site. In addition, you can configure your browser to block or delete cookies at any time. Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of the site.

5. Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookies Policy to reflect legal or technical changes. The current version will always be available on this page. Last update: 2026.
